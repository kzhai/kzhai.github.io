Estimating π via Dart Throwing

A circle of radius 1 sits inside a 2×2 square. Darts land uniformly at random. Since the circle's area is π and the square's area is 4, the fraction of darts inside the circle converges to π/4 — so π ≈ 4 × (inside / total).
